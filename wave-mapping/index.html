Across Nathan Garrett’s books, coherence describes coordination that supports perception and response while allowing difference. Reducing interference creates room to recognize what is already present. This experience offers a small, tangible way to explore that theme.

Coherence, interference, and this example

For these equal-pitch waves, coherence means a stable relative phase. Reinforce and Cancel both have it. Shifting Phase models reduced coherence by varying B’s phase. Different pitches can form an orderly pattern too.

The interference slider adds a separate random disturbance to the combined signal. Reducing it reveals the known signal without changing the sources’ coherence. In physics, interference also includes the reinforcing and cancelling of waves; it is not always an obstacle.

The books use these ideas more broadly to explore human experience. A generated wave is an analogy for that exploration, not a measurement of a person’s inner state.

Book connections: The Frequency of Love, chapter 12; The Physics of Forgiveness, Compassion & Presence, “Why Coherence Matters”; The Master of Energy & The Holy Grail, pp. 102–103. Wave definitions: OpenStax.

Here, the signals are generated, so we know what went into them. In the wider Wave Mapping research, the next question is which patterns in real observations hold up when tested.
