THE SMALLEST PUBLIC EXPRESSION

The Seven-Question Doorway

☺HUMAN
FIRST

Creation OG is not understood by memorizing eleven Modules. It is entered through seven questions that keep a possibility, the Human, and the wider field in truthful relationship.

1

WHO DECIDES?

Who possesses actual authority? Who may consent, refuse, interrupt, appeal, and leave? Who remains responsible?

2

WHAT ARE WE TRYING TO CREATE—AND WHY?

What Human or living capacity should become more possible? What must this creation never become?

3

WHO AND WHAT ENTERS THE FIELD?

Who participates? Who is affected without participating? What burden may be hidden?

4

WHAT PERMISSION AND AUTHORITY EXIST?

What is freely ours to decide? What requires consent, competence, jurisdiction, or renewed review?

5

WHAT IS THE SMALLEST TRUTHFUL FORM?

What is small enough to correct, real enough to teach, and honest enough not to require inflated claims?

6

HOW WILL THE WORLD REPLY?

What evidence, testimony, consequence, or adverse signal would require correction, repair, revision, or stop?

7

HOW CAN THIS END?

Can authority return? Can people leave? Can data, resources, ownership, obligations, and public association transfer, retire, or release?
