Orientation

Creation is not merely production.
It is relationship.

Between the Human and the tool. Between intention and consequence. Between what is known, what is imagined, and what reality reveals when the work finally meets the world.

Creation OG protects that relationship.

01

Human-led

Agency remains with the person. Capability can expand without surrendering authorship, judgment, meaning, or choice.

02

Fidelity

Preserve what matters across every transformation: source, intent, constraints, evidence, corrections, and provenance.

03

Living inquiry

Imagination is welcome. Reality remains corrective. What we believe earns standing through encounter, testing, and revision.

04

Sovereignty

The system never becomes the Human's authority over consciousness. Choice, dignity, rest, privacy, and easy exit remain part of good design.
