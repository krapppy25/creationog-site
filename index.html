WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU ENTER?

Creation is a living conversation, not a straight line.

1 · Bring what is real.

An idea, question, challenge, decision, hope, observation or possibility.

2 · Change the lens.

Look through different perspectives—Human, relational, evidential, ecological, imaginative and practical.

3 · Notice what appears.

New information, tensions, relationships, opportunities and consequences can become visible.

4 · Try something truthful.

Experiment at the smallest useful scale. Let reality answer back.

5 · Adjust, learn, return.

No shame in changing direction. Restoration may mean becoming something new.
