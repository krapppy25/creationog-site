About this experience

This is a small exploration of sound, language, and attention. Each viewing repeats the same nine-second sound and pattern three times. The second adds descriptive words.

Repetition and familiarity can also change what you notice. This experience invites reflection; it does not isolate or measure an effect caused by words.

The picture is a cymatics-inspired mathematical model, not a recording of sand or water. Its pattern follows the same signals used to make the sound.

Creation OG is a human-led exploration of music, meaning,
