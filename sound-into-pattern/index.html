Coherence, cymatics, and this model

Here, coherence refers to a stable relative phase between two modeled vibration modes. Increasing phase fluctuation varies that relationship over time. The colored regions show lower average motion, including cancellation; a coherent pattern can contain quiet regions.

The sound combines the same two time signals used to calculate the picture. The 432 Hz carrier uses our A432 reference. Phase variation introduces additional frequency components.

This is a simplified square-membrane model, not a simulation of sand movement or a prediction for a particular metal plate or water surface. Physical cymatic patterns also depend on geometry, material, support and how the surface is driven.
